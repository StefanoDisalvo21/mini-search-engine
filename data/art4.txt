"Artificial Intelligence - arXiv" and "Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research" are excellent resources for academic research papers. ArXiv is a repository for pre-print papers in various scientific fields, including AI. The Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research (JAIR) publishes peer-reviewed articles on all areas of AI.
